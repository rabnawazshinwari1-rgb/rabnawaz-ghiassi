01 My story
Who I am

I don't come from an easy background. That's why I build differently.

Born on November 1, 2003, in Baghlan, Afghanistan, and trained as a plumbing and heating engineer, I learned to build with my hands before I learned to build with my ideas. What I learned on job sites — rigor, method, and respect for work done right — I now apply to everything I'm building.

Raised between cultures, I speak French, English, Persian, and Pashto. This isn't a footnote — it's my greatest strength. It lets me reach people that most content creators never do.

"You don't build a life the way you build a house — but both demand the same things: a solid foundation, patience, and work."
